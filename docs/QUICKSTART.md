# 🚀 BuzzAnimate 5-Minute Quickstart Guide

> Learn the essentials of drawing, keyframing, and exporting your first animation in BuzzAnimate in under 5 minutes!

---

## Step 1: Launch BuzzAnimate
1. Double-click **`BuzzAnimate.bat`** in the project folder.
2. A blank 1920×1080 stage opens at 24 frames per second.

---

## Step 2: Draw a Character or Ball (Merge Shapes vs. Object Drawing)
1. Pick the **Oval Tool (`O`)** from the left toolbar.
2. Hold **`Shift`** and drag on the white stage to draw a perfect circle.
3. Switch to the **Selection Tool (`V`)**:
   - Notice how you can hover near the edge of the circle until the cursor shows a curve arc, then drag to bend and squash the shape organically!
4. **Coloring**: Open the **Color** panel on the right. Pick a fill color and click inside the circle with the **Paint Bucket (`K`)**.

---

## Step 3: Animate with Keyframes (Frame-by-Frame)
1. Look at the **Timeline** at the bottom of your screen. Your drawing currently lives on **Frame 1**.
2. Press **`F6`** on your keyboard:
   - This inserts a new **Keyframe** at Frame 2, duplicating your ball.
3. With the Selection tool (`V`), move the ball slightly to the right and downward.
4. Press **`F6`** again for Frame 3, move the ball down further, and squash it slightly against an imaginary floor.
5. Repeat for Frames 4, 5, and 6 to bounce the ball back up.
6. **Previewing**: Press **`Enter`** (or the Play button). Watch your bouncing ball loop continuously!
   - Press **`.`** (period) to step forward one frame.
   - Press **`,`** (comma) to step backward one frame.

---

## Step 4: Turn on Onion Skinning
1. Press **`Alt + Shift + O`** (or click the Onion Skin button on the timeline header).
2. You will see colored ghost silhouettes of previous and upcoming frames.
3. Drag the range handles in the frame ruler to see more or fewer in-betweens while refining your motion arcs.

---

## Step 5: Convert to a Symbol & Tween
1. Select your artwork and press **`F8`** (Convert to Symbol).
2. Name it `BouncingBall` and select **Graphic**.
3. Move the playhead to Frame 24 and press **`F6`**.
4. Move the ball to the other side of the stage.
5. Right-click anywhere between Frame 1 and Frame 24 on the timeline and choose **Create Classic Tween**.
6. Press **`Enter`**: BuzzAnimate automatically calculates all the smooth in-between frames for you!

---

## Step 6: Add Studio Vector Lighting
1. Open the **Lighting** panel on the right (`Window ▸ Lighting`).
2. Click **Add Lamp**.
3. A warm interactive lamp appears on the stage!
4. Drag the lamp's central gizmo to move it around your character. Watch how surface shading and cast shadows automatically update in real time!
5. Toggle light handles on or off at any time using **`Ctrl + Shift + L`**.

---

## Step 7: Export Your Animation
1. Choose **`File ▸ Export ▸ Export Video…`** or **`Export Image…`**.
2. For video, select **H.264 (MP4)** or **ProRes 4444 (.mov)**.
3. Choose your destination file and click **Export**.
4. Your export renders in the background without locking the interface. Monitor progress in the **Tasks panel (`Window ▸ Tasks`)**.
