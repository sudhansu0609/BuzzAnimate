 22	## 1. What it is
 23	
 24	GPU-accelerated vector animation — Adobe Animate's workflow rebuilt on a modern
 25	engine. Not a new kind of tool: an Animate user should be productive on day one.
 26	The improvements go **underneath** — unbounded zoom, all cores, GPU rasterisation
 27	— not on top, where the buttons are.
 28	
 29	**Stack:** Rust · wgpu · Vello · egui · `f64` geometry throughout via `kurbo`.
 30	
 31	| | Adobe Animate 2024 | BuzzAnimate |
 32	|---|---|---|
 33	| Maximum zoom | 2 000% | **no cap** — verified to 2×10¹⁴% |
 34	| CPU | effectively single-threaded | **work-stealing pool, all cores** |
 35	| Rasterisation | CPU | **GPU compute shaders (Vello)** |
 36	
 37	**16 crates, ~48 000 lines.**
 38	
 39	| Crate | Does |
 40	|---|---|
 41	| `buzz-geom` | `f64` geometry and the rebasing camera behind unbounded zoom |
 42	| `buzz-scene` | Copy-on-write document model, Animate-compatible layers, R-tree index |
 43	| `buzz-doc` | The `.buzz` format, undo history, autosave and crash recovery |
 44	| `buzz-render` | GPU device selection and Vello-backed rendering |
 45	| `buzz-jobs` | Work-stealing job system and utilisation metrics |
 46	| `buzz-ui` | Theme, menus, panels, stage chrome |
 47	| `buzz-app` | The application shell |
 48	| `buzz-rig` | Armatures, IK, skinning, mesh warping |
 49	| `buzz-light` | Sun, sky and lamp; shading and cast shadows |
 50	| `buzz-fx` | Filters and blend modes, as vector geometry |
 51	| `buzz-audio` | Decoding, waveforms, playback, lip-sync analysis |
 52	| `buzz-export` | Finished frames out: PNG images and sequences, MP4/MOV via NVENC |
 53	| `buzz-script` | Sandboxed JavaScript API over the document |
 54	| `buzz-import-xfl` · `-swf` · `-pdf` | The three importers |

 73	
 74	### Drawing
 75	
 76	- **23 tools** — Selection, Subselection, Lasso, Magic Wand, Free Transform,
 77	  Gradient Transform, Pen, Text, Line, Rectangle, Oval, PolyStar, Pencil, Brush,
 78	  Bone, Asset Warp, Paint Bucket, Ink Bottle, Eyedropper, Eraser, Camera, Hand,
 79	  Zoom — on Animate's own letters. **22 of them work; Text is inert** (§7-9), and
 80	  a test asserts it is the only one.
 81	- **4 brush kinds** — Fluid, Pattern, Art, Soft (raster) — with **7 pattern
 82	  shapes** including one made from a selection.
 83	- Gradients: linear and radial, on fills and strokes, with a working Gradient
 84	  Transform tool.
 85	- Shape recognition (circles, ovals, squares, rectangles at any angle), smoothing,
 86	  straightening, boolean operations, path offsetting.
 87	- Bitmaps as a paint; Lasso that *cuts* the artwork it crosses; Magic Wand.
 88	
 89	### Document & timeline
 90	
 91	- **7 layer kinds** — Normal, Folder, Mask, **Inverse Mask**, Masked, Guide, Guided.
 92	- Layer parenting, per-layer depth, colour, transparency, outline view, lock, hide.
 93	- **Cut, Copy and Paste** — artwork carries the symbols it needs and moves between
 94	  documents. **Align, Distribute and Match Size** in `Modify ▸ Align`, with or without
 95	  the stage as the frame. **Arrow-key nudge**, one unit and eight with Shift.
 96	- Timeline with Animate's frame conventions, onion skin, Edit Multiple Frames,
 97	  Auto Keyframe, a looping section, two zooms, and layer switches on the rows.
 98	- **Tweens** — classic, motion and shape, with easing in the model.
 99	- Symbols (Graphic, MovieClip, Button), nested timelines, a Library with folders,
100	  search, usage counts and **a thumbnail of every symbol**.
101	- Camera with keyframes, spatial 3D rotation, layer depth and a real projection.
102	- Undo/redo by snapshot; autosave; crash recovery offered on launch.
103	
104	### Look
105	
106	- **5 filters** — Blur, Drop Shadow, Glow, Bevel, Colour Adjust.
107	- **10 blend modes** — Normal, Layer, Darken, Multiply, Lighten, Screen, Overlay,
108	  Hard Light, Add, Difference.
109	- **Lighting** — sun, sky and lamp, with shading, highlights and cast shadows.
110	  A deliberate addition; Animate has no lights (§7-44).
111	- Named swatches in folders; dark and light themes.
112	
113	### Sound
114	
115	- Decodes `.wav` `.mp3` `.ogg` `.flac` `.m4a` `.aac`.
116	- A soundtrack is audible from inside any symbol at any depth, draws its waveform
117	  in the timeline, and drives **automatic lip sync**.
118	
119	### Rigging
120	
121	- Bone tool; armatures over shapes and symbol chains.
122	- **FABRIK IK** with angle limits and pin constraints.
123	- Puppet warp via Moving Least Squares; vertex weight binding; poses that tween.
124	- **A pose library on each rig** — name a pose, apply it, mirror it, or *Key* it so
125	  two poses tween; it travels with the character into any document. Bones can be
126	  deleted and reparented.
127	- Budget: 50 six-bone rigs solved in parallel, well inside one frame.
128	
129	### In and out
130	
131	| Direction | Formats |
132	|---|---|
133	| **Reads** | `.buzz` · `.fla` · `.xfl` · `.swf` · `.pdf` · `.ai` |
134	| **Writes** | `.buzz` · PNG image · PNG sequence · **MP4 / MOV, with the soundtrack muxed in** |
135	
136	Video encodes on the GPU through NVENC (`h264_nvenc` / `hevc_nvenc` / `av1_nvenc`)
137	with software fallbacks, driving the ffmpeg already on the machine — frames are piped
138	as they render, so a 500-frame export never lands 4 GB of PNG on the disk.
139	
140	All three importers merge into an **already-open** document, remapping every id,
141	and report what they could not bring across.
142	
143	### Scripting
144	
145	Sandboxed JavaScript through Animate's own `fl` / `document` API — rectangles,
146	ovals, layers, frames, selection, the library and document properties.

150	## 3. Restrictions
151	
152	Four kinds, and the difference matters: some of these will never change, some are
153	waiting on other people, some are simply not built yet, and some are choices.
154	
155	### 3.1 Hard limits — architectural, not going away soon
156	
157	| Limit | Why | Ref |
158	|---|---|---|
159	| **egui pinned at 0.35** | 0.36 needs wgpu 30; vello 0.9 needs wgpu 29. Two wgpu majors cannot share a device. **Blocked on vello.** | §7-2 |
160	| **`f64` precision floor** | Sub-pixel accuracy to ~1e12%, linear decay after. By design. | §7-4 |
161	| **Filters and lighting are geometry, not raster passes** | Vello offers no shader hook, so a shaded side is a boolean crescent and a blur is built from bands. Keeps everything editable; will not match a true raster blur. Holds for *per-shape* effects only — a **full-frame** pass is possible at the seam where Vello's output is blitted, which is what `ARCHITECTURE.md` Wave 6 builds on. | §7-46, §7-52 |
162	| **No tablet pressure** | winit 0.30 supplies none on Windows. The brush reads pressure and the setting exists; every sample arrives at 1.0. | §7-25 |
163	| **Legacy binary `.fla`** (CS4 and older, OLE2) | Out of scope. | §7-5 |
164	| **No `.fla` write-back** | Import only. Not planned for v1. | §7-6 |
165	| **egui is immediate-mode** | Chosen to reach a working app fast; acknowledged as not ideal long-term for a pro tool. | §7-3 |
166	| **Windows-only launcher** | `BuzzAnimate.bat` is a batch file; elsewhere it is `cargo run --release -p buzz-app`. | §7-106 |
167	
168	### 3.2 Not built yet — the significant absences
169	
170	| Missing | Consequence | Ref |
171	|---|---|---|
172	| **Text tool** | No titles or credits without drawing them by hand or importing them. Needs font loading, shaping and an editing caret. | §7-9 |
173	| **HTML5 export** | PNG, PNG sequence, MP4/MOV, GIF and WebP are the outputs; a self-playing HTML5 build is not. | CP-6.4 |
174	| **Thumbnails in the Assets panel** | The Library draws a picture of every symbol; an asset is a file on disk, so a picture of one needs a background read. | §7-81 |
175	| **Motion Editor, motion paths, shape hints** | Easing exists in the model and interpolates; nothing edits the curve, and a tween cannot follow a drawn path. | §7-18 |
176	| **Bitmap import in all three readers** | XFL, SWF and PDF count and report their bitmaps as skipped. The pipeline they need now exists. | §7-158, §7-116 |
177	| **Multiple scenes** | One scene per document. | §7-12 |
178	| **AS3 runtime and compiler** | JavaScript scripting works; ActionScript does not exist. | CP-8.3–8.4 |
179	| **Bézier pen and the Bind tool** | The Pen draws segments; skin weights cannot be painted by hand. Bones can now be deleted and reparented. | §7-11, §7-34 |
180	| **Depth of field** | Layers off the focal plane stay perfectly sharp. | §7-29 |
181	
182	### 3.3 Deliberate deviations from Animate
183	
184	Each is off by default or additive, so a document that does not use it behaves
185	exactly as Animate would.
186	
187	| Deviation | Ref |
188	|---|---|
189	| **Lighting** — Animate has no lights at all | §7-44 |
190	| **Build-up paint** — Animate's shapes always composite source-over | §7-27 |
191	| **Inverse mask** — a `.fla` cannot express one, so a document using it will not round-trip | §7-119 |
192	| **The looping section** lives in the document and the exporter repeats it; Animate's loop is a transport setting | §7-66 |
193	| **Auto Keyframe** — Animate has no such mode | §7-70 |
194	| **Named swatches in folders** — Animate's panel is a flat grid of unnamed chips | §7-76 |
195	| **The Assets panel** — Animate's ships curated content and syncs with Creative Cloud | §7-80 |
196	| **Panels move by menu, not by dragging** — grouping into tabs now works (§7-57 resolved); dragging a panel into a dock does not | §7-56 |
197	| **One workspace**, plus Reset — Animate saves named workspaces | §7-58 |
198	| **Two themes, not a theme editor** | §7-89 |

210	## 4. Suggested improvements
211	
212	The full argument, with sizing and what already exists to build on, is in
213	[`IMPROVEMENTS.md`](IMPROVEMENTS.md). In brief:
214	
215	> **The diagnosis: the tool is built for drawing, and the work being done is
216	> assembly** — placing characters you already own, into stages you have already
217	> built, in poses you have already worked out. Almost none of what makes that slow
218	> is hard. It is missing.
219	
220	### Wave 1 — stop retyping work already done
221	
222	| | Size | |
223	|---|---|---|
224	| **The clipboard** | M | `Scene::extract` and `Scene::merge` both already exist and are tested. Mostly wiring. |
225	| **Thumbnails** | L | Off-thread raster into a cache keyed by symbol and revision. Turns choosing-by-name into choosing-by-sight. |
226	| **Drag from panel, drop on stage** | S | Removes two steps from every placement. |
227	
228	### Wave 2 — poses become things you own
229	
230	**Pose Library** (M) stored on the `Symbol` so it travels with the character ·
231	**Mirror a pose** (S) · **Pose-to-pose keying** (S), which turns a pose library
232	into a way of *animating* · **Bone delete and reparent** (M).
233	
234	### Wave 3 — set the stage once
235	
236	**Scene templates** (M) · **Align and Distribute** (S) · **Arrow-key nudge** (S) ·
237	**Live transform preview** (S–M).
238	
239	### And the beautiful half
240	
241	**Motion Editor and motion paths** (L, §7-18) — the single biggest available
242	change to how motion *feels* · **Keyframed lights** (M, §7-47) · **Depth of
243	field** (M, §7-29) · **The Text tool** (L, §7-9).
244	
245	### Recommended order
246	
247	```
248	Clipboard  →  Thumbnails  →  Drag-to-place
249	```
250	
251	All three are absences rather than hard problems; all three are repaid every time
252	a character is placed; and thumbnails are what makes the Pose Library worth
253	building at all — a pose you cannot see is a pose you will not reuse.
254	
255	### Beyond that: Parts II and III
256	
257	Part I above makes the tool quicker. **Part II changes what it is** — one program for
258	vector *and* raster drawing, exporting in the background while you start the next shot,
259	lit and composited like a 3D package, on an engine with one rule: *the window never
260	stops responding.* Designs in [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md).
261	
262	| Wave | | Size |
263	|---|---|---|
264	| 4 | Foundations — the task registry; everything long comes off the UI thread | M |
265	| 5 | Background export — queue, presets, Tasks panel, GIF/WebP | M |
266	| 6 | Compositor — bloom, grain, vignette, grade; cheap depth of field | M |
267	| 7 | **Raster layers** — paint properly, beside the vectors | **L** |
268	| 8 | Asset pipeline — watched folders | S |
269	| 9 | 2.5D — keyframed lights, depth sorting, real depth of field | M+S+M |
270	| 10 | The film — `.buzzproj`, many shots into one movie | M |
271	| 10b | **Camera angles** — stage once, shoot from anywhere | S–M |
272	
273	**Part III** is the delight half: motion trails and arcs, audio scrubbing, a video
274	reference layer for rotoscoping; the **procedural modifier stack** (springs on bones =
275	automatic follow-through); stabiliser, symmetry and perspective guides; true motion
276	blur and alpha video at export; a command palette.

297	## 6. Keeping this file true
298	
299	`OVERVIEW.md` is a **summary of the other files** and holds no facts of its own.
300	When something ships:
301	
302	1. `PROGRESS.md` §4 gets a section saying what was built and **why it was built
303	   that way**.
304	2. Its `§7-nn` row is struck through and marked resolved.
305	3. Its row in `IMPROVEMENTS.md` is struck through with a pointer to that section,
306	   and — for a Part II or III wave — the design in `ARCHITECTURE.md` is marked
307	   shipped, with any difference between design and build recorded in §4.
308	4. **Then** the relevant table here is moved — out of §3 and into §2, or out of §4.
309	
310	If this file and `PROGRESS.md` ever disagree, `PROGRESS.md` is right.
