# Trace a picture as it is (2026-11-01)

User: "the trace is not working — when I click trace it shall trace the image
exactly as it is." Open document: one picture, `ComfyUI_0265.jpg`, 5120 × 2880,
shown at 1920 × 1080 (a painted night street).

## Measured (before)

| Route | Result | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Modify ▸ Trace Bitmap (quick: Outline, 6 colours) | black-and-white line drawing, "2 colours"; mean RGB error 269 | 3 s |
| Trace Bitmap… dialog (saved: Auto, 24 colours, Drop background on) | Auto → colour *lines*; Drop background deleted the street (most common colour) → white | **152–166 s UI freeze** (log, three times) |

Error = mean Euclidean RGB distance to the source at 1920 × 1080 (0–441).

## Causes

1. Quick trace asks for 6 colours; the palette collapsed to 2 on a dark scene.
2. Tracer `Auto` sends a colour picture to `ColourLines` (dark strokes over the
   regions): error 9.7 vs 4.4 for plain `Outline`, and the slow path.
3. `trace_with` traces the full 5120 px source on the UI thread, though the
   stage shows 2.7 source px per unit.
4. The dialog's remembered `drop_background` (set for characters on paper)
   drops the colour covering the most area — on a scene that is the street.

## Round 2 (user, 2026-11-02, testing by hand)

"At 700–800 % I see white vacant unfilled specks" and "anything with alpha is
traced as solid colour".

Measured: `trace_png src1920.png colour:64` rendered at 8× over magenta
(crop 560,300–680,380): **0.115 % of pixels are background showing through**,
all hairline slivers along shared edges (brick mortar, awning edge). Cause: in
`trace_regions` (`buzz-scene/src/trace.rs` ~744) every region's outline is
traced and smoothed independently (`build_region_shape` → `outline_colour`), so
two neighbours never share one edge; a 0.2–0.5 px gap becomes 2–4 screen px at
800 %. The renderer's seam seal is a fixed screen hairline, so it cannot close
zoom-scaled gaps. (A global `expand_fill(0.5)` was tried and left commented at
~969.)

Alpha: `trace_regions` treats `alpha < 128` as transparent (not traced) and
every other pixel as fully opaque; the palette and the fill colour ignore alpha
(~604, `region_fill_colour`).
