## Decisions (Opus)

- **Setting:** keep the story, move it to a poor rural village (UP, 2011,
  Choti Diwali night): mud-and-brick shops, khaprail roofs, kachchi gali,
  diyas, load-shedding, a CFL on a 12 V battery. The "sheet-ghar" is the
  village's abandoned cold storage behind the shop. People are poor villagers.
  Names, dialogue and plot unchanged.
- **Style:** the Khayal3Baje LoRA workflow + the profile's `style_text` (2D
  painted, desaturated, bold black outlines). Strip photo words from the
  user's prompts (`photorealistic`, `35mm`, `film grain`, `8k`, `macro`,
  `practical`) and append the style text; keep everything else they wrote.
  Night, moody, horror — never "light humour".
- **Angles:** the user's 3 angle prompts per scene are the plates. Each
  screenplay scene uses its story scene's 3 plates as shots (wide / medium /
  close as fits the angle), cutting between them on the lines.
  **Plates are empty** (no people), so characters stand on them.
- **Layers:** every plate → `split_plates` + objects (`--objects`): Sky / Far /
  Mid / Ground layers and each object (door, counter, trash mound, car, wall…)
  as one symbol on its own layer above its plate, so a character can go behind
  it.
- **Cast (dummy):** stand-ins from the user's library (`cast_sources`), no
  generated characters: Deepak (38, poor shopkeeper), Bhushan (40), Mishra Ji
  (58, heavy moneylender), Radhe (48, drunk), Alakshmi (entity: thin, dark,
  tall — any dark/thin female rig, flagged as a stand-in), Padosi 1 & 2,
  Narrator (voice only).
- **Audio:** none yet. Lines timed from text at a slow Hindi narration rate
  (~2.2 words/s, horror pauses). Each scene gets empty `Voice` and `SFX` sound
  layers with a marker on each line/SFX cue, so the user's recordings drop in
  (later: `--audio-parts`).
- **Life:** text lip sync, blinks, eye moves (look at whoever speaks), breathing,
  talk gestures (hands), entrances, lights (inverter CFL warm, moon cold,
  headlights, dawn) — the producer's existing steps, all on.
- **Parts:** ~10 parts of 150–180 s (measured from the built timelines), each
  a group of whole screenplay scenes; a story scene may be split in two at a
  line boundary to balance. Output `part_01.buzz` … plus `Alakshmi.buzz`
  (all scenes).
